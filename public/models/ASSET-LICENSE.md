The GLB is unchanged. Runtime modifications: scale and forward-axis normalization, chassis-relative vertical offset, cloned paint/glass/trim materials, emissive lighting, and recentered front-wheel geometry reused for four independently animated wheels. Source rear wheels are not rendered because they share one mesh. Source geometry is cached and shared; per-car materials are disposed on unmount. No external textures or runtime network services are required.

All track textures, curbs, trees, lamps, particles and synthetic audio are generated in project code. The inspected Kenney pack was not integrated into the shipped game.
